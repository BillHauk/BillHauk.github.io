I study **international trade, economic growth, political economics,** and the intersection of **Catholic social thought and economics** — how political institutions and economic incentives shape countries' trade policies and development.

My work has appeared in the *Journal of Economic Growth*, the *Journal of Comparative Economics*, the *Quarterly Journal of Political Science*, and the *Journal of Macroeconomics*, among others. I also write for [The Conversation](/media/) and comment regularly on trade and inflation for national and regional press.
